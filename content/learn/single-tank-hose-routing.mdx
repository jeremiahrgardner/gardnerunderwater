Look at a typical rental regulator. A yellow octopus on a long loop. A console swinging off the side. Hoses pointing every direction.

It works. Until you need it to work fast.

The problem isn't the gear. It's that nothing lives in a fixed place. And a regulator you have to look for is a regulator you can't count on.

## What does the single tank layout look like?

One first stage. Every hose has a job and a place.

| Hose | Side | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-hose primary (7 ft) | Right | Down your right side, across your chest, around your neck |
| Necklace backup | Left | To the necklace under your chin |
| Wing inflator | Left | To the inflator on your left shoulder |
| Pressure gauge | Left | Down your left side to the left hip D-ring |
| Drysuit inflator | Whichever runs cleanest | Under your arm to the suit valve |

No octopus. No console. Nothing looping out from your outline.

## Why does the long hose go on the right?

Because of what happens next.

A teammate is out of gas. You donate the regulator you're breathing, on the long hose, and it presents straight to them. Your backup is right under your chin. The whole layout is built around those few seconds. See [Out-of-Air Drill](/learn/out-of-air-drill-long-hose).

## Why does it have to be the same on everyone?

Because a team reads each other's gear. If your gauge, your backup and your long hose are exactly where your teammate's are, they can find them on you, in bad visibility, without asking.

That's what the layout is really for. Not neatness. Predictability.

## Is this overkill on a single tank?

Let's be honest. A lot of divers think DIR configuration is for tech divers. Doubles. Caves. Not a Saturday at La Jolla Shores.

**The comfortable belief:** I'm a recreational diver, so a recreational setup is fine.

**The truth:** an out-of-gas emergency doesn't check your certification card first.

And here's the part people miss. The single tank is where you build the habit. The layout you learn on one first stage is the one you'll use on [doubles](/learn/doubles-hose-routing). Learn it once. Then just keep adding. [DIR for Recreational Divers](/learn/dir-for-recreational-divers) makes the case in full.

## Can you set it up from memory?

Pull your regulator out of the bag tonight. Where does each hose go? Why there? Which hand reaches for which regulator, and when?
